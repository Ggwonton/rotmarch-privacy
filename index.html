Rotmarch: Descent into the Abyss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Rotmarch is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your saved game

Your progress (such as your hero, items, pets, companions and settings) is saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The game does not request any special permissions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
